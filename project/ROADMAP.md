# Roadmap AI Growth Engine

## Phase 0 — Foundation

Проектная память, рабочая ветка, backend skeleton, database, health API, integration registry и локальный запуск.

## Phase 1 — Market Data

Wordstat API, seed generation из текущих услуг, raw query storage, demand history.

## Phase 2 — AI Intelligence

GigaChat provider, normalization, intents, semantic clustering, relevance, opportunity detection.

## Phase 3 — Decision Engine

Opportunity scoring, allowed action types, `WATCH` / `DO_NOTHING`, reasoning summary, evidence, confidence.

## Phase 4 — Content Actions

Article, service, landing и FAQ drafts; home promotion; internal links.

## Phase 5 — Approval UI

`/ai`: opportunities, actions, preview, approve/reject.

## Phase 6 — Publication

Только после отдельного решения владельца.

## Phase 7 — Feedback Loop

Webmaster, Metrika, measurement и AI evaluation результатов.

## Phase 8 — Autopilot

Только после успешного тестирования approval mode.
